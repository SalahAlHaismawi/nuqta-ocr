Best Arabic OCR models in 2026, compared

Scores from the two public Arabic OCR benchmarks, KITAB-Bench and Misraj-DocOCR, for open models, cloud APIs and classic OCR engines. Other systems' numbers are the ones published by the benchmark authors.

Summary

  • Most accurate overall: Nuqta-Arabic-OCR 4B (open, Apache-2.0). First on both benchmarks.
  • Best small model: Nuqta-Arabic-OCR 0.8B. Second on KITAB-Bench, ahead of Gemini-2.0-Flash and GPT-4o, and runs on a phone.
  • Lowest character error on Misraj-DocOCR: Baseer v2 (0.12) and Nuqta 4B (0.121) are effectively tied; Baseer v2 has no public weights.
  • Classic engines: Tesseract (0.54) and EasyOCR (0.58) miss about half of the characters on KITAB-Bench.

Questions

Which Arabic OCR is the most accurate in 2026?

Nuqta-Arabic-OCR 4B has the best published score on both public Arabic OCR benchmarks: KITAB-Bench character error rate 0.106 and Misraj-DocOCR MARS 82.8.

Is Tesseract good for Arabic?

Tesseract works for clean printed Arabic but misses many characters on real documents: 0.54 character error rate on KITAB-Bench, against 0.106 for Nuqta-Arabic-OCR 4B. It also drops or mangles tashkeel.

Is Gemini or GPT better than open models for Arabic OCR?

Not on these benchmarks. Gemini-2.0-Flash scores 0.13 and GPT-4o 0.31 on KITAB-Bench, and Gemini-2.5-Pro 70.8 and GPT-5 54.8 MARS on Misraj-DocOCR, all behind Nuqta's open models.

What is the best Arabic OCR for tables?

On Misraj-DocOCR tables (TEDS), Nuqta-Arabic-OCR 4B scores 76.1, ahead of Baseer v2 (67), Gemini-2.5-Pro (52) and Azure (42).
